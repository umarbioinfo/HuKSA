# KLIFS and PKIDB atlas-excluded benchmark

This is a retrospectively defined external benchmark, not a prospectively locked test set. It contains 125 compounds with activity-backed kinase labels from KLIFS or curated targets from PKIDB.

Compounds matching the 794-compound HuKSA atlas by canonical SMILES, full InChIKey, or the first InChIKey block were excluded before evaluation. Canonical-tautomer overlap was checked separately. Target recovery was evaluated at identical top-1, top-3, and top-10 cutoffs.
